The original Ogg file is bundled unchanged. Playback and analysis decode it
to mono at the browser's sample rate. Individual-tone buttons select excerpts
at 0.400-1.250, 1.700-2.650, 3.050-4.050, and 4.500-5.250 seconds.
Downloaded WAV copies contain decoded mono samples and, if selected, an
excerpt. These adaptations of the recording remain CC BY-SA 4.0. WAV INFO
metadata retains the author, source, license, and excerpt bounds.
